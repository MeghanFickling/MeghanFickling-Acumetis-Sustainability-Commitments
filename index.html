Our Planet

Measuring our impact. Setting science-based goals. Taking action.

Climate action begins with measurement.

We can’t improve what we don’t measure. Each year, Acumetis measures its greenhouse gas emissions, identifies the activities that contribute most to our footprint, and develops strategies to reduce them.

This includes improving energy efficiency, reducing waste, and working with suppliers that support responsible environmental practices.

Our environmental priorities

  • Measuring and reporting our greenhouse gas emissions annually
  • Improving energy efficiency across our offices
  • Reducing waste and promoting responsible waste management
  • Working with suppliers that support sustainable business practices

Science-based climate action

To ensure our climate goals align with the latest climate science, Acumetis has committed to the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi).

What does the SBTi validate?

Corporate climate targets. The SBTi validates corporate climate targets to ensure they align with the latest climate science and support limiting global warming to 1.5°C.

Our People

Creating a workplace where people can thrive.

At Acumetis, sustainability means more than protecting the environment. It’s about creating a workplace where people feel supported, connected, and empowered to do their best work.

We believe that when we invest in our people, we build a stronger, more resilient business for the future.

Learn & Grow

We encourage continuous learning by providing employees with opportunities to build new skills, develop professionally, and grow their careers.

  • Individual Development Plans, regular performance feedback, and clearly defined career pathways to support professional growth.
  • An Educational Assistance Program that helps eligible employees pursue approved external education by reimbursing qualifying tuition and course expenses.
  • Conferences, workshops, and professional development opportunities.
  • Education and training through the Academy and company-wide learning sessions.

Connect & Engage

A sustainable workplace is one where employees feel connected to one another and to our shared purpose.

  • Weekly team lunches, office celebrations, and social events across our global locations.
  • Company-wide initiatives such as our annual June Fitness Challenge and other wellness activities.
  • Volunteer opportunities and employee engagement initiatives that support the communities where we live and work.
  • Interest-based Microsoft Teams communities where employees can connect over food, sports, books, and other shared interests.

Belong & Be Supported

We are committed to creating an inclusive, respectful workplace where everyone can succeed.

  • Equal Opportunity and Anti-Harassment policies that foster a safe and respectful workplace.
  • Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion initiatives and celebrations, including Pride Month and International Women’s Day.
  • Flexible ways of working that support work-life balance across our global offices.
  • Employee recognition through our Kudos program and appreciation initiatives.
  • A collaborative culture where employees are encouraged to share ideas, ask questions, and engage directly with leadership.

Our Communities
